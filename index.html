🔍 Defect Inspector

Upload a photo of any object (cup, jug, bottle, pen, tool...). AI looks for damage; it also builds a 3D model with the defects marked.

    AI visual judgement from one photo, not a measured result. Check anything important in person.

    Approximate 3D: your photo is wrapped around round objects and shown on both faces of boxes and other shapes, with estimated thickness. Hidden sides are guesses. Drag to rotate.

    Your photo is sent to Google's Gemini API to be analysed. On the free tier Google may use it to improve its models, so please don't upload private or customer photos.
